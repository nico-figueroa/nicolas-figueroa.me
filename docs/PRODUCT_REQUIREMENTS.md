# Product Requirements

## Project

Nicolas Figueroa Professional Portfolio

## Domain

nicolas-figueroa.me

---

# Product Vision

Create a modern, AI-enhanced professional portfolio that allows visitors to explore Nicolas Figueroa's professional journey, experience, projects, and personal story through the lens of systems thinking.

The website should act as both:

1. Professional portfolio
2. Interactive exploration platform

The website should communicate:

"Exploring how complex systems learn, adapt, and evolve."

---

# Success Criteria

A visitor should be able to:

- Understand who Nicolas is
- Review experience
- Evaluate projects
- Learn his professional philosophy
- Ask questions through AI Guide
- Evaluate role fit through Check Nico's Fit

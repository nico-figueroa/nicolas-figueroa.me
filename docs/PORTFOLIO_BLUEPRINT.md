# Vision

Create an AI-guided professional portfolio centered around the theme:

> Exploring how complex systems learn, adapt, and evolve.

The website should communicate who Nicolas is, what he has built, how he thinks, and how he approaches learning and solving complex problems.

The website is not intended to function as a traditional portfolio.

It is intended to function as an explorable knowledge network that allows visitors to understand Nicolas's professional experience, personal journey, technical capabilities, projects, and growth trajectory.

The AI components are intended to guide discovery, not replace it.

The website must remain fully useful without AI features.

# Growth Projection Engine

For every identified skill gap, estimate an individual learning timeline at
beginner (foundational familiarity), intermediate (independent implementation
and troubleshooting), and advanced (design, optimization, and mentoring-level
depth). Also provide an overall estimate for acquiring all the identified
skills at each proficiency level; the overall estimate assumes the skills are
studied sequentially. The exact gap list must match the skills listed in every
level of the projection.

Historical calibration may use the MS in Information Management completed
while working full time with a 4.0 GPA, the Full Stack certification completed
in approximately 3 months, the AI Engineering certification completed in
approximately 1 month, and other documented learning milestones. These
outcomes inform estimates but do not guarantee similar results for a different
skill.

Use an explicit part-time study assumption of approximately 6–8 focused hours
per week. Show the projection as a reasoned estimate, not a prediction or
guarantee, and explain its assumptions.
